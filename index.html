The video brews 30 g to 500 g at 60 g per litre (≈1:16.7). Changing the dose or ratio recalculates the water, keeps the bloom at 2× the coffee and the first pour at 60% of the water. The clock times stay the video's.

Before you start

  1. Boil your water.Hottest water you can — especially with lighter roasts.
  2. Grind 30 g of coffee medium-fine.
  3. Rinse the paper filter with water just off the boil.Dump the rinse water from your server.
  4. Add the grounds to the V60.
  5. Make a well in the middle of the grounds.
  6. Tare your scale and have the kettle in hand.Hit Start the moment you begin the bloom pour.

    Notes from the video

    • Swirl, don't stir, the bloom. Swirling wets all the grounds more effectively than a spoon.
    • Keep the V60 fairly full during the first main pour — it helps hold temperature.
    • Don't worry about pouring onto the paper.
    • Second pour is gentler. A little slower than the first; don't pour aggressively.
    • The final swirl flattens the bed so the coffee extracts evenly as it drains.
    • Drawdown speed is affected by water temperature and your choice of filter paper.
    Method: James Hoffmann, “The Ultimate V60 Technique” (2019).
