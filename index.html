    Before you start

    0 of 6+
    1. Boil your water.As hot as you can — especially with lighter roasts.
    2. Grind 30 g of coffee, medium-fine.
    3. Rinse the paper filter with water just off the boil.Dump the rinse water.
    4. Add the grounds to the V60.
    5. Make a well in the middle of the grounds.
    6. Tare the scale, kettle in hand.Press Start as you begin the bloom pour.
